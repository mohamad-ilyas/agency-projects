# Playbook — James Whitaker, Senior Software Architect & Technical Lead
Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Standards we follow
- **Record every important decision as an ADR** (context, options, decision, consequences) in `docs/decisions/`. MADR 4.0.0 (2024-09-17) is the current template; it has full and minimal variants. Nygard's original template is the alternative. Source: [adr.github.io/madr](https://adr.github.io/madr), [adr.github.io/adr-templates](https://adr.github.io/adr-templates).
- **Design against the OWASP Top 10:2025** (announced Nov 2025; final released Jan 2026 per a search summary). Categories confirmed in sources: A01 Broken Access Control (now includes SSRF), A02 Security Misconfiguration, A03 Software Supply Chain Failures (new), A04 Cryptographic Failures, A05 Injection, A06 Insecure Design, A07 Authentication Failures, A08 Software or Data Integrity Failures, A09 Security Logging & Alerting Failures, A10 Mishandling of Exceptional Conditions (new). Sources: [OWASP Top 10:2025 introduction](https://owasp.org/Top10/2025/0x00_2025-Introduction/), [eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/threats/news-owasp-top-10-2025/), [Orca Security](https://orca.security/resources/blog/owasp-top-10-2025-key-changes/). Rule for us: A03 and A10 become explicit review items (dependency and workflow trust; safe failure on malformed input).
- **Treat a public broker as untrusted input.** Every message is validated at the boundary (schema, size, ranges, clock skew, plausibility), not only shape-checked. Application-level integrity checks or payload encryption are the options when the transport has no authentication; TLS alone protects only the link. Source: [HiveMQ, MQTT message data integrity](https://hivemq.com/blog/mqtt-security-fundamentals-mqtt-message-data-integrity), [HiveMQ, payload encryption](https://www.hivemq.com/blog/mqtt-security-fundamentals-payload-encryption/). (Undated pages; read as standing guidance.)
- **Keep the transport behind an interface** (as in xo-online: `Transport` with MQTT, BroadcastChannel and in-memory implementations) so the trust model can change without rewriting the app. This is our own practice, not an external source.
- **Simplicity and $0 first.** Pick the least complex option that meets the NFRs; any paid service goes to the COO.

## Lessons from our projects
- **xo-online (review 2026-11-01/02): a public MQTT broker means every client is a possible attacker.** `parseRoom` first only checked shape; the reviewer showed that huge `version`, far-future timestamps and inconsistent boards could freeze rooms or flood the lobby (F-02). Fix pattern now in the code: bound version and timestamps, check board/turn consistency, cap list and cache sizes, accept the residual (anti-cheat out of scope) in writing. Next time, put these bounds in the first ADR, not after review.
- **Never let a client's local clock authorise destructive actions** (F-03): a viewer with a clock far ahead could clear other people's live rooms. Only seated players may clear; clocks are compared with a sanity bound. The new 10-minute skew limit has a side effect (N-01): a device whose clock is far behind silently ignores its opponent. Design the user-facing message for a rejected-but-valid state.
- **Say in the ADR when we accept a risk, and who accepted it.** F-04 (public broker, open lobby) stayed open until the CEO accepted assumptions A1-A6 via the COO.
- **Evidence over claims:** tests ran only against an in-memory broker, so live behaviour (Last Will, reconnect, latency) stayed "unverified" until a real two-device check. Mark such ADR consequences as unverified.
- **Change of meaning needs a recorded decision** (N-04): a fix changed the AC-7.2 "idle" semantics without a note in the spec. Record behavioural changes made during security fixes in the ADR or spec.
- **todo-app (review 2026-11-01):** storage input is validated with a type guard and nothing is rendered as HTML (no `innerHTML`/`eval`), which is the pattern to repeat; the review's findings were Low and process-related (lint not in CI, stale report text).
- **Release automation is code too:** the xo-online release workflow failed at its report step on a heredoc error. Test workflow steps locally before relying on them.
